Privacy Policy · Last updated: 22 October 2026

1. What we collect at sign-in

When you sign in with Google, the application receives your name and email address, only to identify you and to check whether you are on the approved-users list. It does not read your Gmail, contacts or other Google account content.

2. Case data you upload

Files you upload (such as bank statements, call records and portal reports) are processed to produce analysis and reports. The application is designed to process this data within your own browser session. We do not sell, share or use it for advertising.

3. Backups

If the backup feature is used, backup files are saved only to your own Google Drive account, under your control, and not to any other location.

4. Storage and security

Access is restricted to approved users. Sensitive data should not be kept in the browser longer than needed; clear your session when you finish. Please keep your Google account secure, as access depends on it.

5. Sharing

We do not disclose your personal information to third parties, except where required by law.

6. Your choices

You can stop using the application at any time and can ask for your email to be removed from the approved-users list by contacting the administrator.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

8. Contact
